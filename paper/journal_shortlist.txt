Target journal shortlist

Tier 1: best fit
1. Journal of Cheminformatics
   - Best scope match for chemical structure representation, structure/similarity search, QSAR, data mining, and cheminformatics methodology.
   - Best first submission target for this manuscript.
   - Scope: https://jcheminform.github.io/jcheminform-author-guidelines/aimsAndScope.html

2. Journal of Chemical Information and Modeling (JCIM)
   - Strong fit if the paper is framed as a methodological chemical-informatics contribution with clear validation and reusable scoring workflow.
   - Scope: https://pubs.acs.org/page/jcisd8/about.html

3. Molecular Informatics
   - Good fit if the manuscript is framed as molecular informatics / computer-assisted molecular design rather than only software deployment.
   - Scope: https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/page/journal/18681751/homepage/productinformation.html

Tier 2: conditional fit
4. BMC Chemistry
   - Broad chemistry venue; acceptable if the manuscript is more application-focused and less specialized than JCIM / Journal of Cheminformatics.
   - Scope: https://bmcchem.biomedcentral.com/submission-guidelines/aims-and-scope

5. Computational Toxicology
   - Use only if the paper is reframed toward safety / hazard / toxicology-related chemical grouping rather than broad product-category scoring.
   - Scope: https://www.sciencedirect.com/journal/computational-toxicology

6. Current Computer-Aided Drug Design
   - Use only if the manuscript is reframed toward drug-design relevance; otherwise the product-category scope is broader than this journal’s center of gravity.
   - Scope: https://www.benthamscience.com/journal/5/about-journal

Recommended submission order
1. Journal of Cheminformatics
2. JCIM
3. Molecular Informatics
4. BMC Chemistry

Reason for this order
- The current manuscript is strongest as a cheminformatics methodology paper: full-positive PubChem category rebuilding, property-plus-pattern scoring, and same-regime QED comparison.
- It is weaker as a toxicology-only or drug-design-only story.
