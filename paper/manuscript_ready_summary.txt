Manuscript-ready result package

Primary tables
- final_category_rebuild_summary.csv: final uncapped broad-category scorer results for the 11 selected categories.
- final_category_rebuild_qed_comparison.csv: same categories with direct RDKit QED comparison on the same rebuilt evaluation sets.
- regime_separated_comparison_table.csv: older regime-split comparison for original-vs-hybrid-vs-stress interpretations.
- evidence_table.csv: legacy benchmark evidence references.

Main scientific claim now supported
- The final broad-category rebuild uses all available PubChem positives in this run, retained cross-category negatives, positive-set physicochemical ranges, and structural patterns.
- Every final category now has an explicit same-regime QED comparison.
- All 11 final manuscript categories beat QED in AUC on the final rebuilt evaluation sets.

Positive-count interpretation
- Positive counts in the final tables are unique SMILES counts after PubChem CID-to-SMILES conversion and SMILES deduplication.
- Therefore they are expected to be lower than raw PubChem CID counts for some categories.
