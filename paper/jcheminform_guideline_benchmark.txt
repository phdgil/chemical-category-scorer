Journal of Cheminformatics guideline and article benchmark

Official guidance confirmed
- Research article abstract maximum: 350 words.
- Scientific Contribution section in abstract: maximum 3 sentences.
- No explicit official maximum main-text word count was found in the current Research guidance.
- No explicit official maximum number of figures or tables was found in the current Research guidance.
- Figure title limit: 15 words.
- Figure legend limit: 300 words.
- Table title limit: 15 words.
- Table legend limit: 300 words.

Why article benchmarking matters
Because the journal does not state a hard maximum for main-text length, figures, or tables, the most practical calibration comes from similar accepted articles in Journal of Cheminformatics.
